## FOCUS MAINTENANCE PROTOCOLS

### Context Switching Elimination
- **Task Batching**: Group similar activities (all coding, all writing, all communication)
- **Theme Days**: Monday planning, Tuesday-Wednesday creation, Thursday execution, Friday review
- **Tool Consolidation**: Minimize app switching within work sessions
- **Transition Rituals**: Clear mental shifts between different work types

### Communication Boundaries
- **Designated Windows**: 10am, 2pm, 5pm for messages and email
- **Deep Work Protection**: No interruptions during 90-120 minute blocks
- **Emergency Protocols**: Clear criteria for urgent vs. non-urgent
- **Response Expectations**: Set clear availability and response time standards

### Energy Optimization
- **Peak Performance (8-11 AM)**: Creative work, complex problem-solving, writing
- **Medium Energy (11 AM-2 PM)**: Meetings, communication, routine tasks
- **Low Energy (2-4 PM)**: Administrative work, planning, learning consumption
- **Recovery Energy (4-6 PM)**: Strategic thinking, content consumption, reflection
